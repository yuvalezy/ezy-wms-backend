---
title: Transfer Confirmation Reports
description: Review transfer confirmation data.
---

The Transfer Confirmation reports function identically to the Goods Receipt reports but display transfer confirmation data.

## Available Reports

- **Confirmation Report** — `/transferConfirmationReport`
- **Difference Report** — `/transferConfirmationProcessDifferenceReport/:scanCode`
- **All Items Report** — `/transferConfirmationReportAll/:scanCode`

See [Goods Receipt Reports](/docs/goods-receipt/reports) for detailed report descriptions.
