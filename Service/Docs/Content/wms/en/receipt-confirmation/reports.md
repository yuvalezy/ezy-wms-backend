---
title: Receipt Confirmation Reports
description: Review receipt confirmation data.
---

The Receipt Confirmation reports function identically to the Goods Receipt reports but display confirmation data.

## Available Reports

- **Confirmation Report** — `/goodsReceiptConfirmationReport`
- **vs Exit Report** — `/goodsReceiptConfirmationVSExitReport/:scanCode`
- **Difference Report** — `/goodsReceiptConfirmationProcessDifferenceReport/:scanCode`
- **All Items Report** — `/goodsReceiptConfirmationReportAll/:scanCode`

See [Goods Receipt Reports](/docs/goods-receipt/reports) for detailed report descriptions.
